How it works

One line to install. Three steps to value.

Stratum is transparent — it speaks the Anthropic API, so your existing tools and SDKs don't change. You point them at it once.

1

Point your base URL here

Set ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL to your Stratum endpoint and authenticate with your key. Every request now flows through the gateway — no code changes.

2

We measure & prune

Each turn is token‑counted with the provider's exact usage, the wasted re‑sent context is identified, and (as the engine activates) pruned — with a published <5% quality gate.

3

You pay 20% of savings

Your provider bill drops; Stratum invoices 20% of the measured reduction. No savings, no fee — every dollar is signed and reconcilable in the CFO dashboard.
