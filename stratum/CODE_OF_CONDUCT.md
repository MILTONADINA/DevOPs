# CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md

## Our Commitment

Startum is a technical project with high standards for both code quality and human interaction. We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, professional experience for everyone.

---

## Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Give and receive technical feedback directly and constructively
- Disagree on ideas, not on people
- Back assertions with evidence — benchmarks, papers, measurements
- Acknowledge when you're wrong; update your view when the evidence changes
- Respect that other contributors have different contexts and constraints
- Write ADRs when making significant decisions so reasoning is documented and revisitable

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment of any kind
- Dismissing contributions without technical justification
- Deliberately ignoring the documented rules in `CLAUDE.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md`
- Claiming credit for others' work
- Publishing others' private information without consent

---

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and any communication channel associated with CQ development.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at `conduct@startum.com`. All reports will be reviewed and investigated. The maintainer is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter.

Responses may include: a warning, temporary suspension from participation, or permanent removal from the project.

---

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreements are healthy and expected. The resolution process:

1. Both positions state their case with evidence (benchmarks, papers, code)
2. If unresolved: write competing ADRs and let the eval suite adjudicate where possible
3. If still unresolved: the maintainer makes a call, documents the decision, and moves on

Relitigating settled decisions without new evidence is not productive. If you have new evidence that changes the calculus, open an issue referencing the original ADR.
